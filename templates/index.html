About the Model

Precision Agriculture Powered by Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classifier

AgroPredict uses a Machine Learning-based Random Forest Classifier to recommend the most suitable crop based on soil and environmental conditions. The model analyzes seven important parameters: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), temperature, humidity, soil pH, and rainfall. It is trained on the Crop Recommendation dataset containing different crop categories and their suitable growing conditions. By learning patterns between these parameters and crop requirements, the model predicts the crop that is most suitable for the given conditions. The system is designed to assist farmers and users in making more informed crop selection decisions.
